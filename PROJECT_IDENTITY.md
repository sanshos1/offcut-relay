# Offcut Relay identity

- Mechanism signature: one reusable material offer can receive many independently checked project routes; a compatible route can reserve the exact quantity and produce an attributable handoff record.
- Product metaphor: a communal cutting bench. The material is a measured shape; a recipient lays a project stencil over it.
- Narrative voice: a practical workshop keeper—short measurements, direct cautions, no sustainability theatre.
- Visual grammar: chalk cutting mat, signal orange rulers, recycled paper tickets, dense corner annotations.
- Interaction grammar: measure stock -> open fit drawer -> place stencil -> test fit -> tear off pickup ticket.
- Evidence story: the donor's frozen material sheet is re-fetched for every screening; project and workshop records are bound to their own digests.
- Documentation form: bench rules, measurement marks, and handoff sequence rather than a feature catalog.
